Manufacturing: frequently asked questions

What manufacturing capabilities does Rajpall Manufacturers offer?

Rajpall's capabilities cover precision machined components, springs, rubber components, fasteners, casting and forging products, marine craft, welding wire, drilling products and custom manufacturing.

Can Rajpall manufacture products according to customer specifications?

Where manufacturing capability permits, Rajpall can work around customer requirements such as drawings, dimensions, materials and application specifications. Specific custom-manufacturing capabilities should be confirmed with the Rajpall engineering team.

Does Rajpall manufacture or supply products?

Rajpall Manufacturers LLP operates as a manufacturing and trading company. Depending on the product and customer requirement, it can manufacture products or support procurement through its trading and supply capabilities.
