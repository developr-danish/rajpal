Product overview

Rubber components play a quiet but essential role in vehicles and industrial equipment. They seal joints against leakage and contamination, absorb vibration and shock, and provide flexible connections between mechanical parts. Because the job differs from one application to the next, Rajpall treats automotive rubber products as application-specific components.

Rajpall supplies both moulded and extruded rubber components. Moulded components are formed to a defined shape, while extruded products are produced in a continuous profile, so the right route depends on the shape and function your application needs. Whether the requirement is sealing, vibration control or a mechanical application, the starting point is always your specification.

Rajpall Manufacturers LLP is a manufacturing and trading company serving the automotive, defence, mining and construction industries. Share your drawing, specification or application details, and the Rajpall team will review them and discuss the appropriate manufacturing or supply solution.

Rubber products sit within Rajpall's wider automotive range, alongside springs, bolts and machine components, and specialised bolts, so related requirements can be discussed with one team.
