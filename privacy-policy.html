Introduction

Rajpall Manufacturers LLP ("Rajpall", "we", "us") is a manufacturing and trading company serving the automotive, defence, mining and construction industries. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using this website or submitting an enquiry, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, mainly through our Request a Quote form:

  • Contact details: your full name, company name, business email address and phone number.
  • Enquiry information: your industry, the product or requirement you are asking about, quantity, requirement details and your preferred contact method.
  • Uploaded files: any drawing, specification or other document you attach to your enquiry.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device and pages visited, through cookies and analytics tools (see below).

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • respond to your enquiry and prepare quotations;
  • review your drawings, specifications and requirements;
  • communicate with you by your preferred contact method;
  • manage the business relationship that follows from your enquiry;
  • understand how our website is used so we can improve it; and
  • meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Uploaded files

Files you upload are used to review your requirement and discuss a manufacturing or supply solution. Please upload only what is needed to describe your requirement, and avoid including personal information that is not necessary for that purpose.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We may use cookies that are necessary for the website to work and, where applicable, cookies that help us understand how visitors use the site. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, although some parts of the website may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party services to help operate this website and handle enquiries, such as website hosting, email, form processing and analytics providers. These providers may process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services. We do not sell your personal information.

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information for as long as it is needed to respond to you, manage the resulting business relationship and meet legal or accounting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it; and
  • object to or ask us to limit certain uses of your information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
