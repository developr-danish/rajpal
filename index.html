Frequently asked questions

What does Rajpall Manufacturers manufacture?

Rajpall Manufacturers LLP manufactures and trades precision components and engineered products serving the automotive, defence, mining and construction industries. Its portfolio includes springs, rubber products, bolts and machine components, marine products, welding wire, casting and forging products, drill bits, drill spares and drilling tools and consumables.

Which industries does Rajpall Manufacturers serve?

Rajpall Manufacturers serves the automotive, defence, mining and construction industries. Its products are intended for applications where reliability, precision, durability and consistent performance are important.

Is Rajpall Manufacturers a manufacturer or supplier?

Rajpall Manufacturers LLP operates as a manufacturing and trading company. Depending on the product and customer requirement, the company can manufacture products or support procurement through its trading and supply capabilities.

Can Rajpall manufacture products according to customer specifications?

Where manufacturing capability permits, Rajpall can work around customer requirements such as drawings, dimensions, materials and application specifications. Specific custom-manufacturing capabilities should be confirmed with the Rajpall engineering team.

Which products does Rajpall offer for defence applications?

The defence portfolio includes inflatable boats, fibre glass boats, bolts and machine components, bogie bolts, ER70S-7 MIG welding wire, and casting and forging products.

How can I request a quote?

Share your requirement through the Request a Quote form, including the product, quantity and application details. You can also upload a drawing or specification so the Rajpall team can review it.

Looking for a Manufacturing Partner?

Tell us about your component, product or application requirement. Our team can review your specifications and discuss the appropriate manufacturing or supply solution.
